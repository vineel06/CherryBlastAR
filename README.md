🍒 CherryBlastAR
CherryBlastAR is an immersive augmented reality ( OpenCV ) shooting game where your hand becomes the controller. 
Use a simple pinch gesture to pop balloons, rack up points, and avoid deadly bombs!

---

🎮 Gameplay Overview
Turn your fingers into a virtual gun:
- Aim with your index finger  
- Pinch your thumb and index finger together to shoot  
- Pop balloons to score points  
- Avoid bombs — one hit and it’s game over  

⚡ Challenge increases every 5 points** as balloon speed ramps up!

---

✨ Features
🖐️ Smart Hand Interaction
- Real-time Hand Tracking powered by MediaPipe  
- Pinch-to-Shoot Mechanic for intuitive gameplay  

🎯 Game Modes
- Single Player – Classic balloon shooting  
- Multiplayer – Split-screen competitive mode  
- Time Challenge – Choose between:
  - 1 minute  
  - 2 minutes  
  - 5 minutes  

🔥 Dynamic Gameplay
- **Progressive Difficulty** – Faster balloons every 5 points  
- **Bomb Balloons** – Black balloons labeled *“BOMB”* end the game instantly  

🎨 Visual & Audio Effects
- Balloon glow effects  
- Particle explosions on hit  
- Sound effects:
  - 🎈 Pop (normal balloons)  
  - 💥 Explosion (bombs)  

💾 Progress Tracking
- Persistent **High Score System** (saved across sessions)

⚙️ Performance
- Optimized for smooth gameplay (tested on RTX 3050)  
- Fully responsive via webcam input  

---

🕹️ How to Play
🎯 Controls
- Shoot: Pinch thumb + index finger  
- Aim: Move your index finger  
- Crosshair turns 🔴 *red* while pinching  

📋 Menu Controls
- Press `1` → Single Player  
- Press `2` → Multiplayer  
- Press `3` → Time Challenge  
  - Then press `1`, `2`, or `3` to select duration  
- Press `Q` → Quit anytime  

🔁 Game Over
- Press `R` → Restart (returns to menu)  
- Press `Q` → Quit  

---

🧰 Requirements
- Python **3.7 – 3.11**  
- OpenCV  
- MediaPipe  
- NumPy  
- Pygame *(optional, for sound effects)*
